Seat Desk — a front desk that answers in Hindi and English, and refuses to guess.

A small study library's front desk answers the same questions all day: क्या कल दोपहर सीट खाली है? What is the monthly fee? When do you open? Seat Desk answers them in Hindi or English. The demo runs on synthetic data modelled on Rasra Library & Study Point.

The desk answering a seat question

The design rule: the model understands, the code decides

AI assistants fail front desks when they invent facts. Seat Desk splits the job: the model reads the question, and code supplies every fact.

🧠 Understanding

Meta's Muse Spark 1.3 turns a sentence — "28 अक्टूबर को दोपहर में कौन सी सीटें खाली हैं?" — into a validated, structured query (date, time slot, intent). It does not write the answer.

🎙 Spoken Hindi

Muse Voice Transcribe 1.0 turns a recording into a transcript, which is used only after the speaker confirms or corrects it. Built into the local desk; a successful live transcription is not yet on record.

🧮 Facts

Seat availability, hours and fees come from deterministic code and an approved knowledge pack (synthetic in the demo). A policy answer must cite an approved entry, or it goes to a person.

🤝 Honest limits

Refunds, bookings and anything outside the approved pack produce a handoff summary for a person, marked not sent. Nothing is booked or messaged automatically.

Architecture in one paragraph

Voice or text enters a local web desk (plain Python, no frameworks). Audio goes to Muse Voice Transcribe over Meta Model API; the transcript must be confirmed by the speaker. Confirmed text goes to Muse Spark 1.3 with a strict JSON schema, minimal reasoning effort, bounded output tokens, zero retries and one outstanding request. The typed, validated extraction (language, intent, date, interval, slot, seat) drives a deterministic availability engine and an approved-policy retrieval layer with citation checks, and code writes the answer. In the test suite and the one-shot live harness, an independent checker re-derives the expected interval, counts and free seats from the raw fixtures, so a wrong count, interval or seat set is recorded as a failure. Every response is labelled with its provenance: synthetic data, live or mock understanding, transport, and token usage in live mode.

Demonstration data is historical and synthetic (fixture clock 27 October 2026 IST). No real member records exist anywhere in the project, and the real library's membership system is not connected.
