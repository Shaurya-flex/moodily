What AI does well

  • Explains anything in plain words
  • Drafts messages, letters and applications
  • Translates between languages

What can go wrong — and how this guide protects you

  • It can invent facts, sources and prices that sound true

The safety system

The 7 things to never share with AI — codes, PINs, card details, IDs, medical records. Print it, keep it by your phone.

Verify habit

For anything about money, health or law, treat AI's answer as a first draft and confirm the fact on the official source.

Human control

AI drafts; you decide. Nothing in this guide asks AI to move money, diagnose, or make the final call.

A sample workflow

Workflow 7 of 25 · Health prep

Prepare questions before a doctor visit

The problem. Your appointment is in two days. You want the most from 10–15 minutes but don't know what to ask.

Copy-ready prompt.

I'm seeing my doctor in 2 days about [condition]. My current medications are [names — doses not needed]. My main concern is [worry]. Prepare 6 short, specific questions I should ask, ordered from most to least important.

Safety: AI does not diagnose or prescribe — it only helps you prepare. Never start or stop a medicine on AI's word.
