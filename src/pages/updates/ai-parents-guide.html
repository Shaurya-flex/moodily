Important tool changes to re-check

AI tools change their free tiers, limits, model names and prices frequently. Before relying on any tool detail in the guide, confirm the current state on the provider’s own site:

  • Free tiers & limits — the free plans of ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Google Translate (and Bhashini, India edition) are enough for the guide’s workflows, but exact limits shift over time.
  • Model names & features — voice, image and web-browsing features move between tiers; use whatever your app currently shows.
  • Prices — any paid-tier price mentioned is indicative only; check the provider’s pricing page for today’s number.

Safety updates

The STOP page and scam-safety rules are evergreen: never share OTPs/codes, PINs, passwords, card CVV, bank logins, government IDs, or private medical records with AI — or anyone who contacts you. If a message or call makes you feel rushed, scared, or lucky, stop and verify through an official channel. Any new scam patterns worth flagging will be added here.
