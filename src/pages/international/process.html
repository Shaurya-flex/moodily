The rules we work by

Your accounts stay yours

Profiles, ad accounts, analytics and domains stay in your name. We work through manager or delegated access and never ask for your passwords. You can remove our access at any time.

Revisions are defined

Each service includes a set number of revision rounds. One round is a single, consolidated set of changes. New requirements are quoted separately.

Timelines start with inputs

Typical timelines begin once we have complete information, assets and access. Platform verification can extend the final go-live date.

Ownership on full payment

Final deliverables are yours once the project is paid in full. We keep our own templates, tools and methods, and show your work only with your written permission.

What we need from you

Your side

  • One person who can approve decisions
  • Access to the accounts in scope, in your name
  • Content, photos and facts we can't invent
  • Feedback within the agreed review window

What delays a project

  • Missing access or content
  • Approvals that take longer than agreed
  • Changes to scope after approval
  • Platform reviews outside our control
