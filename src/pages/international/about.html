How a project runs

Three things you can rely on, whatever the size of the job.

1

Scope

Before any work or payment you get a written scope: deliverables, what isn't included, revision rounds, timeline, price and what's needed from you. Nothing starts until you approve it.

2

Feedback

You review at the points set in your scope. Send one consolidated set of changes per round; anything beyond the scope is quoted before it's done.

3

Handover

Final files and accounts stay in your name. Moodily never asks for passwords, and you can remove its access at any time.

Working principles

  • AI-assisted, human-reviewed. AI helps with drafting and research; every deliverable is reviewed by a person and facts are checked against their source.
  • No guaranteed-results claims. No promises of rankings, leads or revenue — only the work agreed in writing, and a plain account of what's outside anyone's control.
  • Public information only for clinics. No patient records, no medical details in forms, no compliance claims.
  • Across time zones. Replies within 24 hours on working days (India Standard Time, UTC+5:30). Most projects run by email, with video calls at agreed review points; WhatsApp if you prefer.
