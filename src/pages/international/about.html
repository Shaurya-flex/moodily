How we work

Written scope, always

Deliverables, exclusions, revisions, timeline and price are agreed in writing before any project starts.

AI-assisted, human-reviewed

AI helps us draft and research faster. A person reviews every deliverable, and factual content is checked against its source.

You stay in control

Accounts stay in your name. We work through delegated access and never ask for passwords.

No guaranteed-results claims

We don't promise rankings, leads or revenue. We commit to the work we agree and say plainly what's outside our control.

Working together across time zones

We work on India Standard Time (UTC+5:30) and reply within 24 hours on working days. Most projects run by email, with video calls at agreed review points. WhatsApp is available if you prefer it.
