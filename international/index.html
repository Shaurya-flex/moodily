The problem

Customers drop off at small, fixable steps

Most businesses don't lose people at one big moment. They lose them between finding you and getting in touch — and again after they buy.

  1. Discover

    Search, a profile, a referral link.Often lost: unclear who it's for

  2. Trust

    Proof, people, policies.Often lost: no visible proof

  3. Understand

    What it is and how it works.Often lost: jargon, no walkthrough

  4. Enquire or book

    The mobile path to act.Often lost: too many steps

  5. Onboard & learn

    The first days after yes.Often lost: no guidance

  6. Return

    Repeat use and referrals.Often lost: silence after the sale

Process

Scope first, then deposit, then work

Projects are never bought from a button. Each one starts with a written scope you approve.

  1. 1

    Tell us what you need

    Send a short request with your goal, timeline and budget range.

  2. 2

    We review the journey

    We look at your current website, profiles and contact path before suggesting anything.

  3. 3

    Receive a written scope

    Deliverables, exclusions, revisions, timeline, price and what you provide.

  4. 4

    Approve the project

    Nothing starts until you approve the scope in writing.

  5. 5

    Pay the deposit

    Work begins once the deposit is confirmed.

  6. 6

    Build

    AI-assisted drafts, reviewed by a person before you see them.

  7. 7

    Review

    You review at agreed points, within the included revision rounds.

  8. 8

    Final payment

    The balance is due before final handover.

  9. 9

    Handover

    Files, access in your name and a short guide to keep things running.

  10. 10

    Optional ongoing support

    Digital Care, if you'd like steady improvements afterwards.

Questions

Who is Moodily International for?

Service businesses, educators, consultants and software teams that need customers to find them, trust them, get in touch and understand what they offer.

Do you guarantee rankings, leads or revenue?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee those. We commit to the deliverables, timeline and revision rounds in your written scope, and we tell you plainly when something is outside our control.

Where are you based and how do we work together?

Moodily is based in India and works remotely in English. Communication is by email or video call; WhatsApp is optional. Times are in India Standard Time (UTC+5:30).

How do payments work?

Fixed-price services are confirmed in writing before you pay. Custom projects receive a written scope and quote; work starts after you approve it and pay the deposit. Prices are in USD unless stated otherwise.

Do you use AI?

Yes, to draft, research and speed up production. Every deliverable is reviewed by a person before you see it, and factual content is checked against its source.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes. Tell us in your request and we'll use your NDA or a mutual one before you share anything confidential.
