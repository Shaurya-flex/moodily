Questions

Do you need access to our product?

A test or sandbox account is ideal. We don't need production customer data, and we avoid showing real customer information on screen.

Who checks that tutorials are accurate?

We check each step against the product before recording, and your subject-matter expert approves scripts at an agreed checkpoint. Nothing is published without your approval.

Can you update tutorials when the product changes?

Yes, as a small follow-up project or within a Digital Care plan with a defined monthly scope.

Can you show our work in your portfolio?

Only with your written permission. Some deliverables can't be shared because of ownership or confidentiality, and that's fine.

Tell us where your customers get stuck.

We'll reply with a suggested scope and whether a first tutorial or a pack makes more sense.
