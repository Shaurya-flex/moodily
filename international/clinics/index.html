Our rules for healthcare work

Public information only

Business profile, website, booking journey, educational content, public FAQs, marketing, analytics and customer communication. We don't access clinical systems or patient records.

Clinicians approve clinical wording

We write and design. Anything clinical is approved by your team. We don't give clinical advice, promise outcomes or use "best" claims.

Your country's rules apply

Advertising rules for health professions differ by country, including how reviews and testimonials may be used. We work within the rules your team confirms.
