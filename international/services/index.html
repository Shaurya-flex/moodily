Questions about services

Who owns the accounts and files?

You do. Profiles, ad accounts, analytics and domains stay in your name; we work through delegated or manager access. Final deliverables are yours once the project is paid in full.

Why don't you need my passwords?

Google, Meta and most platforms let you grant access without sharing a password. That keeps you in control, and you can remove our access at any time.

What counts as a revision round?

One round is a single, consolidated set of changes sent together. Each service includes a fixed number of rounds; new requirements or out-of-scope changes are quoted separately.

What delays a project?

Usually missing content or access, approvals that take longer than agreed, or platform verification. We'll flag anything that affects the timeline early.
