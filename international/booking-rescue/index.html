Booking & No-Show Rescue

An empty slot costs the same as a busy one.

Most booking software can send reminders, hold deposits and stop double-bookings. Most of it is never set up that way. We configure what you already pay for — calendar, reminders, deposit rules, the booking link — so your week fills up with less phone tag and fewer people forgetting they booked.

Start your enquiry

One-off fixed price · 5–7 working days · you keep every account in your own name

This is for you if

You already take bookings. The problem is not getting found — it is what happens after someone decides to come.

  • People book, then forget — and you only find out when nobody arrives.
  • Your day goes on calls and messages agreeing times that software could agree for you.
  • You have a booking tool, but it was never set up past the defaults.
  • You would like deposits on bigger appointments, and have not set them up.
  • Your booking link is hard to find, or you do not have one to send.

How it works

  1. 1

    Tell us

    Send the enquiry form with your business, your booking tool if you have one, and how you work.

  2. 2

    Scope

    We confirm what we would change, in writing, with the price. You pay only after you agree it.

  3. 3

    Configure

    We set up the calendar, reminders, deposit rules and booking link on your own account.

  4. 4

    Hand over

    A 30-minute call and a one-page written guide, so your team can run it without us.

The payment method for your project is confirmed in writing with your scope or invoice.

Work starts once the agreed payment is confirmed. We never ask for card or bank details by email, chat or message.

What we will not tell you

The things worth saying before you spend anything.

  • We will not promise a no-show rate. We configure the system; your customers decide whether they turn up. Anyone quoting you a percentage is guessing.
  • We do not sell you the software. The licence is billed to you by the vendor, in your name, and most of them include onboarding free. We charge for configuration, not for access.
  • We never handle patient or medical data. For clinics we configure the tool; the account, the data and any agreement with the vendor stay yours.
  • If your tool already does this well, we will say so and point you at the setting rather than invoice you.

Ongoing upkeep — reminder tweaks, seasonal hours, reporting — is Digital Care, billed monthly and cancellable. It is optional and never assumed.
