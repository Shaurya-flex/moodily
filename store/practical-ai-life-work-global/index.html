Why AI helps — and what can go wrong

What AI does well

  • Explains anything in plain words
  • Drafts messages, letters and applications
  • Translates between languages
  • Summarises long documents in seconds
  • Prepares your questions before a meeting or a doctor visit

What can go wrong — and how this guide protects you

  • It can invent facts, sources and prices that sound true
  • It can give confident but wrong medical or legal advice
  • Scammers use AI voices and messages to pressure you

Every workflow ends with a human check, and the guide opens with a safety page you can print.

The safety system

STOP page

The 7 things to never share with AI — codes, PINs, card details, IDs, medical records. Print it, keep it by your phone.

Verify habit

For anything about money, health or law, treat AI's answer as a first draft and confirm the fact on the official source.

Human control

AI drafts; you decide. Nothing in this guide asks AI to move money, diagnose, or make the final call.

A sample workflow

Workflow 7 of 25 · Health prep

Prepare questions before a doctor visit

The problem. Your appointment is in two days. You want the most from 10–15 minutes but don't know what to ask.

Copy-ready prompt.

I'm seeing my doctor in 2 days about [condition]. My current medications are [names — doses not needed]. My main concern is [worry]. Prepare 6 short, specific questions I should ask, ordered from most to least important.

Safety: AI does not diagnose or prescribe — it only helps you prepare. Never start or stop a medicine on AI's word.

Frequently asked questions

What is Practical AI for Everyday Life & Work?

It is a plain-English digital guide (PDF) that shows you 25 real, everyday things you can do with free AI tools — from summarising documents and drafting messages to preparing for a doctor visit and spotting scams. Each task comes with a copy-ready prompt, a safety note, and a step where a human checks the result. It is written for everyday people, not techies.

Who is this guide for?

Anyone who wants to use AI confidently in daily life and work — beginners, working professionals, job seekers, small-business owners, parents, older adults and caregivers. No prior AI experience is needed. If you can use WhatsApp or email, you can use this guide.

Do I need to pay for AI tools to use it?

No. The guide is built around a $0 free-tool stack — the free tiers of ChatGPT, Google Gemini and Google Translate are enough for every workflow. You never need to buy anything to follow along.

Yes — safety is the backbone of the guide. It opens with a STOP page (7 things to never share with AI), teaches a verification habit for anything involving money, health or law, and includes a scam-safety system and printable checklists. Its core principle is: AI drafts, humans decide.
