FAQ

Who owns the final product?

You do. Deliverables are created for you and rights transfer on full payment, as written in the agreement. You must own or have permission for the material you send.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We work in English and Hindi, remotely, with quotes in INR or USD.

Will the content be AI-generated?

AI helps with structuring and first drafts. Your expertise is the source, and every product goes through an accuracy review with you before delivery.
