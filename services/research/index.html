Evidence you can weigh

Every finding in a Moodily brief carries one label.

FACT

Directly supported by a cited source.

INFERENCE

A reasoned conclusion drawn from facts.

HYPOTHESIS

A testable idea that needs validation.

UNVERIFIED

Found, but could not be confirmed.

What a decision brief contains

  1. The decision question and scope
  2. One-page executive summary with a recommendation
  3. Findings, each labelled and cited
  4. Comparison tables
  5. Risks, unknowns and what would change the recommendation
  6. Source log

FAQ

What do the FACT / INFERENCE / HYPOTHESIS / UNVERIFIED labels mean?

FACT = directly supported by a cited source. INFERENCE = a reasoned conclusion from facts. HYPOTHESIS = a testable idea that needs validation. UNVERIFIED = a claim we found but could not confirm. You always know how much weight each finding can carry.

Do you use AI for research?

Yes, for gathering and organising. Every factual claim in the final brief is checked against a source by a human before delivery.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. Share your NDA before sending confidential material.

Bring us one decision.

Tell us what you need to decide and by when. We reply with scope, timeline and a fixed quote.
