How it works

Brief, scope, build, review, handover

The same five steps for every project, small or large. Nothing goes live without your approval.

  1. 1

    Brief

    Tell us what you do, who your customers are and what you need. A short form, an email or a WhatsApp message is enough.

  2. 2

    Scope

    You get a written scope: deliverables, exclusions, revision rounds, timeline and price. Nothing starts until you approve it.

  3. 3

    Build

    Work happens in agreed stages, in your own accounts through delegated access. We never ask for passwords.

  4. 4

    Review

    You review at agreed points. Consolidated feedback goes into the revision rounds in your scope.

  5. 5

    Handover

    Final files and accounts stay in your name. Exactly what you receive is listed in your scope.

Questions

Who does Moodily work with?

Local businesses in India — shops, clinics, coaching centres and service providers; service businesses outside India, including dental clinics; and software companies that need product tutorials. If you're unsure, describe your business and we'll tell you honestly whether we're a good fit.

Can we work in Hindi?

Yes. Work for Indian businesses can be done in Hindi, English or both. International work is in English.

How are prices set?

Fixed-scope packages for Indian businesses have fixed prices in rupees. International packages are priced in US dollars. Larger or custom work gets a written quote after a short brief, and you approve the scope and price before anything starts.

Can you guarantee more customers or a top Google ranking?

No — nobody honest can. We commit to the work in your written scope: clear pages, profiles, enquiry paths and tutorials. We say plainly what is outside our control.

Who owns the accounts and files?

You do. Google, WhatsApp, domain and social accounts stay in your name. We work through delegated access and never ask for passwords. What you receive at handover is listed in your scope.

Do you use AI?

Yes, for drafting and research, so the work moves faster. A person reviews every deliverable, and facts are checked against their source before anything is published.

Do you handle patient or customer records?

No. For clinics we work only on public-facing pages, links to your existing booking system and education content you approve. Please don't send patient, medical or payment details in any form or message.

How do I start?

Use “Discuss your project” — a short form, WhatsApp or email. We reply within 24 hours on working days with questions or a written scope.
