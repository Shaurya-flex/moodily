# Moodily.in: Indian Market Rate Check (researched Sept 2026)

**How to read this.** All prices are in INR and exclude GST unless noted. A mark of **[page]** means I opened that page and read the price myself. A mark of **[snippet]** means the price came from a search-result summary and I did not open the page. Market ranges are built from the prices I found; I did not make up any numbers. ## 70%-off founding-customer promotion

**Best entry offers.** These have high demand, low delivery cost, fast turnaround and visible before/after results.

1. **Digital Business Starter (₹4,999 → about ₹1,499).** Demand is huge among local shops. Delivery is mostly templates and takes 2–5 days. A GBP before/after (calls, direction requests) makes a strong case study. Even after the discount it's close to IndiaMART setup prices, so it won't make the brand look cheap.
2. **Medical Store Digital Desk (₹9,999 → about ₹2,999).** It targets a specific niche and the SOP and templates can be reused for every store. A result like "orders on WhatsApp per week" is easy to show and helps sell to other chemists.
3. **Professional Authority Pack (₹14,999 → about ₹4,499).** Delivery is writing only, takes about a week, and has no tool costs. The client's LinkedIn profile is a public case study that professionals share, which spreads word of Moodily.
4. *(Optional)* **Creator Content Sprint (₹9,999 → about ₹2,999).** Scripts are reusable and creators promote the work publicly. Include it only if you can deliver 10 scripts at around 3 hours each.

**Exclude.**
- **Digital Saathi Monthly and LinkedIn ghostwriting retainers.** A 70% discount on a recurring service sets a price anchor that is hard to raise later, and the discount compounds every month.
- **AI automation.** It's already below market, tool and debugging costs are real, and support can keep going long after delivery.
- **Knowledge-to-Product.** Already underpriced and takes the most work.
- **Business Growth Launch.** At ₹3,900 you'd be building a website, logo and SEO below the cheapest freelancer's price.
- **Research Intelligence Sprint.** Takes a lot of work and can't be shown publicly as a case study (client work is confidential).
- **Education content.** Priced per unit, and volume orders at 70% off would lose money.

**Caveats.**
- Several figures come from search-result snippets I did not verify (marked [snippet]).
- Fiverr and Upwork blocked direct access.
- JustDial, Sulekha and UrbanCompany returned no usable prices for these services.
- I found no rupee prices for WhatsApp catalogue setup, PYQ analysis or per-chapter worksheets.
